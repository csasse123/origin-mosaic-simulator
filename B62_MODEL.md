# Origin Tutorial Studio B62

Capture a tile and the celestial sphere now share the default 3D scene. The three tutorial views are Telescope, Capture a tile + sky, and RASA optics. Capture, Object and Full sky are camera presets within the same scene; orbit and wheel zoom remain continuous.

## Sky and target display

- The 5,070 HYG catalogue stars and active tile share a display sphere centered on the telescope. Tile centers are projected using spherical angular offsets and agree with the planner's celestial directions. Only the current footprint is drawn in 3D; capture history remains in the 2D mosaic overview.
- All 49 previously embedded target photographs are searchable. Andromeda, the Pleiades, the Veil and Orion have quick buttons. Targets below the local horizon remain available for teaching and are identified as below the horizon.
- The radius represents sky direction, not stellar distance. Object photographs, their position-angle presentation and shallow image relief are illustrative, not calibrated volumetric reconstructions or survey-image astrometry.
- The public entry point is `index.html`; `origin_mosaic_simulator_b62.html` is the identical downloadable, self-contained file. No installation, build step or server is required.

## Preserved calculations and hardware

Normal planning, mount kinematics and the optical surface model are retained. Checks compare 34 original definitions and 16 complete plan snapshots against B55, plus catalogue transformations and 400 optical ray cases. Exterior details, shell removal/transparency, camera dissection, filter drawer, the orange axis logo, SSAG and both AUX cable connections remain available on both mounts.

The optical prescription remains a reconstructed teaching model, not Celestron's proprietary prescription. See [B61_MODEL.md](B61_MODEL.md) for the hardware references and optical limitations, and [B62_VALIDATION.json](B62_VALIDATION.json) for validation evidence.

## Publication boundary

Private log-authoring tools are provided separately in the owner's local Desktop copy. The public page contains no log importer or imported logs. Its 78 embedded images are identical to the metadata-cleaned public B61 images; no image GPS tags are present. The historical, already-published Andromeda and Veil demonstration presets remain unchanged.
